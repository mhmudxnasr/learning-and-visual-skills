## What to transfer

- **Hierarchy with a purpose.** A compact opening gives the question, attribution, and central distinction. Numbered section headings identify conceptual changes. Strong display type, normal-sized explanation, and quieter captions have visibly different roles. Do not demote essential explanation to caption size.
- **A calm reading surface with precise accents.** The reference uses warm white, dark ink, blue structural emphasis, and coral for a focal contrast or caution. Learn these color roles; adapt the actual colors to the material. Avoid repeated colored panels and full-width banners around ordinary prose.
- **Relationships visible at a glance.** Aligned columns expose a distinction; a connected sequence exposes intermediate steps; a chart exposes magnitude. The surrounding prose explains why the relationship matters and retains conditions the visual cannot hold.
- **Rhythm across the whole article.** Move naturally between explanation, the source's example, a useful visual, and its interpretation. A strong opening followed by uninterrupted dense paragraphs is unfinished composition. Equally, a figure after every paragraph is unnecessary. Choose the form at the actual point of difficulty.
- **Richness through understanding.** Keep substantive examples, supporting studies, intermediate reasoning, and limitations. A compact visual can organize them; it does not justify dropping them. Expand a unit when needed instead of shrinking the type to reproduce the reference's page count.

## Complementary strengths from Muse

Its [preserved HTML](../assets/muse-cognitive-attraction.html) is a selective reference, not an endorsement of its scientific wording, completeness, or markup. Keep GPT-5.6's visual hierarchy and add Muse's economy and quick orientation where useful.

| `.thesis`                                | One prominent opening paragraph gives the research question and central distinction immediately.            | Let the reader grasp the argument before the detail. Keep tentative claims tentative; do not copy the title's false either/or framing about the internet.                                |

| `#map .bars`                             | A single ordered overview makes seven quantities comparable without scattering them across separate panels. | Retain the printed values, a shared zero baseline, and a clear scale and denominator. Group by meaning with restrained color; never imply that overlapping categories sum to 100%.       |
| `#cluster table`                         | Row labels retain the starting group sizes—74, 45, and 40—while showing conditional percentages together.   | Put the denominator in the row label and explain the direction of reading. This makes asymmetric percentages intelligible. Add proper table headers and readable narrow-screen reflow.   |

Muse also demonstrates a warm paper surface with dark text and copper/teal accents. That is a useful alternative color relationship when it fits the source, not a new default palette. Its compactness should remove repetition and scattered presentation, never substantive content.

Transfer the design decisions, not the defects: do not reuse its “real causal sequence” claim for a descriptive study, infer measured virality from an added example, or copy its malformed nested ordered list. Use valid semantic structures, stable Arabic terminology, and the existing embedded-font and source-fidelity guidance. Both reference files are examples to learn from, not whole-document templates to combine.

## Build the visual into the explanation

When prose contains a hard relationship, express that relationship in the layout: a contrast becomes aligned cases; a process becomes linked stages with the intermediate actions; quantities become a correctly scaled chart; overlapping categories become a subset explanation or a small justified inline SVG. Narrative and close reading can remain prose-led.

For example, the difference between “36% of threat articles contain disgust” and “68% of disgust articles contain threat” benefits from two clearly labeled starting populations with the corresponding fraction beside each. Explain that the denominator changes. Do not draw proportional circles from those two percentages alone: they do not establish the absolute group sizes. This is richer teaching without fabricated evidence.

Reuse the technique, then author source-specific labels, examples, structure, and CSS. Keep one canonical article, natural Egyptian Arabic, faithful quotations, and explicit editorial additions. These visuals are selectable semantic HTML/CSS and, when justified, accessible inline SVG; this preference does not request generated pictures or change the inert publication contract.

## Preserve the reading experience across formats

Use an Arabic font with suitable real weights, embedded locally where licensed, and isolate Latin notation. The reference's Plex Arabic, compact measure, generous line height, and restrained rules are useful starting decisions, not mandatory branding.

In narrow views, stack comparisons in reading order and retain their labels; do not squeeze dense tables into tiny columns. Use logical CSS properties and flexible tracks. In A4 print, retain the same content and visual identity, keep headings and captions with their material, and let long units continue at meaningful boundaries. Inspect the existing required representative views and fix observed defects; a preserved reference is not proof that every pattern is flawless or appropriate to every source.
